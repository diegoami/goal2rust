# T04 Close the first native hot call tree

- **Kind**: implementation, after T03.
- **Branch**: `task/T04-native-subtree`.
- **Scope**: choose a bounded complete caller/callee tree from the fresh profile;
  the provisional candidate is `0791:2956` → `1771:3216`.
- **Milestones and Done-when**: the full contract in
  [the roadmap](../roadmap.md#2-t04-first-complete-native-hot-subtree).
- **Owns**: `engine/`, `out/T04/`, new routine provenance and necessary CI tests.
- **Preliminary inventory at d3cf46a3**:
  - `geometry.player_unavailable_on_pitch`, retf 4: `args`, `Regs.enter(2)`,
    ES/DI writes and stack flush, a single far call with the pitch record's
    player word at +0xD, resume at `0791:2968`, AL to local -1, leave/store.
  - `attributes.player_unavailable`, retf 2: no yielded guest calls. Uses
    player pointer DS:A91B, player stride 0x14, signed energy +0xE and flag byte
    +0x10. Preserves a 20-byte stack block; zero-energy and positive-energy
    paths write different spans at `1771:3285`. Three bit tests are already
    inlined upstream; retain their final call/frame bytes and DX effects.
  - The caller's only literal guest callee is 3216; neither body has a dynamic
    guest target. Inline helpers, registry metadata and all listeners/sites
    still need the complete implementation-time audit at the new pin.
- **Architectural requirement**: T02's native-body path only finishes leaves;
  its suspended frames currently hold Python generators. Add an explicit Rust
  continuation for native callers through existing issue/return/suspend logic.
  Do not collapse the guest callee into a helper that hides its stack bytes,
  sites, entry count, stop points or listener order. Unported callers and
  variants continue through the Python bridge.
- **Edge cases**: signed energy boundaries, flags and short circuits, multiply
  high word/AH, wrapped player offsets, SP around zero, pre-existing dead stack
  bytes, site/listener callbacks, suspended caller and replay, nondefault
  variants falling back, and body/caller-return mutants.
- **Before implementation**: confirm candidate and inventory against T03's
  accepted oracle and profile. The old-pin profile is diagnostic, not a reason
  to skip this step or the acceptance gates.
